🎨 Favicon Generator

Generate QR code favicons for Zentopia QR Generator

Instructions:
  1. Choose what text to encode in the QR code
  2. Click the size buttons below to generate each favicon
  3. Right-click each canvas and "Save Image As..."
  4. Save as: favicon-16x16.png, favicon-32x32.png, apple-touch-icon.png
  5. Replace the old favicon files in your project
